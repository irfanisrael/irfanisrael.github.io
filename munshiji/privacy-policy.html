Munshi Ji – Privacy Policy

Har tareekh par saath.

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Munshi Ji ("the app") is a case diary and fee ledger for advocates, published by Irfan Khan ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app handles, where it is stored and how you control it.

In short: you sign in with Google so your diary is safe if you change or lose your phone. Your cases are stored in your private account, which only you can access. Your documents stay on your phone, or in your own Google Drive if you turn on backup. We never sell your data, show ads or use tracking.

1. Account

You sign in with your Google account through Google Firebase Authentication. We receive your name, email address and a user ID from Google. We use them only to sign you in, keep your data in your account and show your name on screen.

2. Case data you enter

The app stores what you type: client names and phone numbers, case details, hearing dates and diary notes, fees, payments and receipts, personal udhar entries and your profile (name, enrolment number, address). This is saved on your phone and synchronised to your private space in Google Cloud Firestore (servers in Mumbai, India), so it is available on any phone where you sign in. Access is locked by security rules: only your signed-in account can read or change it. Other users cannot see it.

3. Documents and photos

Documents and photos you attach to a case are saved in the app's private storage on your phone. If you turn on "Back up to my Google Drive", copies are uploaded to a folder called "Munshi Ji" in your own Google Drive, using Google's drive.file permission. That permission lets the app see only files it created, never the rest of your Drive. These files are stored in your Google account and we do not receive them. If backup is off, documents exist only on your phone and are lost if the app is uninstalled or the phone is reset.

4. Subscription

Paid plans are billed by Google Play. We use RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives your app user ID and the Google Play purchase record, not your card or bank details, which only Google handles.

5. What we do not do

  • No advertising, no analytics or tracking SDKs, no selling or renting of data.
  • We do not read your case data except if you ask us for support and give permission, or if required by law.

6. Permissions

  • Camera: only when you choose "Take photo" for a case.
  • Notifications: for hearing reminders, scheduled on your phone.
  • Files / photos picker: only the files you choose to attach.
  • Internet: to sign in, sync your account, back up documents to your Drive and open links you tap (WhatsApp, eCourts).

7. Sharing that you control

When you send a receipt, reminder, report or backup file, the app opens WhatsApp or your phone's share menu with the content you chose. What you send and to whom is your decision. Those apps handle it under their own policies.

8. Service providers

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore), Google LLC
  • Google Drive (only your own Drive, only if you turn on backup)
  • Google Play Billing and RevenueCat, Inc. (subscription status)

9. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest on Google's servers. On the phone it is kept in the app's private storage. Please use a screen lock on your phone.

10. Retention and deletion

Your data is kept while your account exists. You can delete everything at any time in the app: Profile → Delete my account. This permanently removes your account and all case data from our cloud and from the phone. Files in your own Google Drive stay under your control and you can delete them from Drive. You can also request deletion by email (below) from the email address of your account.

11. Your rights

You can access, correct, export (Profile → Backup file) and delete your data, in line with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

12. Children

The app is for legal professionals aged 18 and above.

13. Changes

We will update this page and the date above if this policy changes.
