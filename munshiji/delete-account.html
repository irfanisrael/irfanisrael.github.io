Delete your Munshi Ji account

Munshi Ji: Advocate Case Diary is published on Google Play by Irfan.Israel (Irfan Khan, Jaipur, India).

Option 1: in the app (instant)

  1. Open Munshi Ji and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

Option 2: by email

Send an email from the Google account you use in the app to irfanisrael@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my Munshi Ji account”. We delete it within 7 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted: your account, profile, all cases, hearings, fees, receipts, udhar entries and settings stored in our cloud (Google Firebase). Nothing is kept after deletion.
What is not deleted by us: files you chose to back up to your own Google Drive (folder “Munshi Ji”) — you control and can delete them in Drive. An active Google Play subscription must be cancelled in the Play Store (Payments & subscriptions).
